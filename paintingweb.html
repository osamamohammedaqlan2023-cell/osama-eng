We add special wall textures and unique designs to give your living room a luxury style.

Primary (Light Blue): Represents calmness, trust, and relaxation. It is often used in bedrooms or offices because it helps with focus and creates a clean, spacious feeling.
Secondary (Muted Gray): Represents neutrality, modernity, and balance. It is considered a modern staple used to create calm backgrounds that highlight furniture and decor without straining the eye.
Success (Soft Green): Represents nature, vitality, and visual comfort. It evokes a sense of growth and renewal, and is ideal for spaces that require positive energy and relaxation, such as living rooms.
Danger (Pink or Light Red): Represents passion, warmth, or high energy. In paints, it is used very cautiously—often on just a single accent wall to break the monotony—because it draws strong attention and high energy.
Warning (Light Yellow): Represents brightness, cheerfulness, and solar energy. It provides visual illumination and conveys warmth and optimism, commonly used in kitchens or bright spaces.
Info (Light Cyan): Represents serenity, freshness, and purity. Resembling clear skies or water, it is used to give a sense of openness and good ventilation in tight spaces.
Light (Off-White / Light Grayish White): Represents purity, simplicity, and spaciousness. It is one of the most popular paint colors because it reflects light brilliantly, making small spaces look larger and more open.
Dark (Dark Gray / Charcoal): Represents luxury, drama, and boldness. It is used to add an elegant, contemporary touch, frequently applied to a featured accent wall to showcase sophisticated details.
